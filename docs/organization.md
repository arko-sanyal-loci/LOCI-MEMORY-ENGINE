## Decision rights

| Decision | Responsible contributor | Accountable approver |
|---|---|---|
| Explore, draft, or implement an assigned change | Assigned human or AI agent | Human task owner |
| Accept a design or benchmark claim | Evidence collector and independent reviewer | Human product owner |
| Merge, deploy, publish, spend, sign, or contact customers | Authorized human operator | Founder/project owner or separately documented delegate |
| Change data retention, access, or safety policy | Engineering proposal and impact review | Human project owner |

No agent may create or imply delegated legal authority through a name, title,
registry entry, or generated document.
